Privacy Policy — Video Cutter

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Video Cutter ("the app") is developed by Native Coder. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or usage data. It has no account system.

Videos

You choose a video with the system picker. Previewing and trimming happen entirely on your device, and your videos are never uploaded to any server. On Android 10 and later, trimmed videos are saved to your device's Movies/Video Cutter folder; on older versions they are stored in the app's own folder. Trimmed videos are shared only to the apps you choose.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your photo or video library. It only reads the video you select, and on iOS it asks for add-only Photos access when you choose to save a video there.

Third parties

The app contains no ads, analytics or third-party tracking services. An optional link to our Audio Trimmer app opens its Google Play page only when you tap it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
